# Dictionary of Philological Anachronisms

## "Идиом" (Idiom)
- **Status**: Preferred in modern academic linguistics (Zaliznyak, Shkolnikov).
- **Context**: Used to avoid the political connotations of "dialect" or "language".
- **Rule**: Use instead of "диалект" when referring to neutral linguistic entities in the 11th-15th centuries.

## "Древнерусский" (Old Russian)
- **Status**: Acceptable but requires temporal framing.
- **Context**: In 14th century contexts, specify "Late Old Russian" or "Early Middle Russian" if dialectal features are present.
- **Rule**: Always check for "Новгородский" (Novgorod) specific features before using the general "Древнерусский" label.

## "Берестяная грамота" (Birch Bark Manuscript)
- **Status**: Standard term.
- **Context**: Refers specifically to documents on birch bark.
- **Rule**: Do not confuse with "пергамен" (parchment) documents which follow different orthographic systems (Bookish vs. Everyday).
